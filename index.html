WorkProof

Prove what was done.

WorkProof is an iPhone and iPad app for field professionals: open a job as a quote — photo of the place, what needs to be done, the price — do the work, close it with an after photo and the client's sign-off, and send a clear, professional PDF report.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no servers, no analytics. Your photos, clients, prices and reports never leave your phone.

How it works

1 · Create a job: before photo, scope, price, planned materials.
2 · Do the work.
3 · Finish: what was done, materials used, after photo with a guided camera overlay, optional client signature.
4 · The PDF report opens by itself — share it with the client in one tap.
