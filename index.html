WorkProof

Prove what was done.

WorkProof is an iPhone and iPad app for field professionals: open a job with a photo of the place, what needs to be done, your price and the materials, do the work, close it with an after photo and the client's sign-off, and send a clear, professional PDF report.

Download on the App Store

Your job data stays on your device. No account, no usage analytics. Your photos, clients, prices and reports never leave your phone.

How it works

1 · Create a job on one screen: before photo, what needs doing, your price and the planned materials.
2 · Do the work.
3 · Finish: what was done, materials used, after photo with a guided camera overlay, optional client signature.
4 · The PDF report opens by itself — share it with the client in one tap.

Free and Pro

Your first two completed jobs are free, with up to five work areas each — photos, client signatures and PDF reports included. WorkProof Pro is an optional subscription that removes both limits and adds your logo, Proof Pack export, job templates and more. Choose the annual plan with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or the monthly plan; the app shows the price of each before you subscribe. See the Terms of Use for subscription details.
