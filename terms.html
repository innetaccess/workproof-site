What WorkProof is

WorkProof is a documentation tool. It helps you record the state of a job before and after work, the work performed, materials, prices, and a client's acknowledgement, and produce a PDF report from those records. Reports reflect what you photographed and entered; WorkProof does not verify your work, does not provide legal advice, and does not guarantee any legal or commercial outcome.
